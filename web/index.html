What "cannot decide" costs, measured

840 claims written by other people, run through this kernel. The refusals are not a bug report: they are the coverage boundary, published with its size.

What this corpus actually covers

A refusal is a coverage boundary, not a judgement that the claim is false. Browse every card below, or jump here from any "undetermined" result; every refusal also hands you the search strings that would settle it.

What this is: a product demonstration of evidence consistency and refusal. A verdict means consistency given this built-in corpus, not truth, and it is not an authoritative fact-check. "Undetermined" does not mean the claim is false. Every snippet is a human-written paraphrase — open the source link to verify the original. The AI contrast above replays real recorded API calls; this static page does not call a model live. Examples are public common claims plus labelled synthetic demos; no private data is used.
